## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-integrity, institutional digital standard tailored for the Department of Social Welfare and Development (DSWD) Assistance to Individuals in Crisis Situation (AICS) online portal. Citizens accessing this portal are often navigating urgent distress, medical crises, bereavement, or disaster recovery. Consequently, the user interface rejects extraneous decorative trends in favor of deep clarity, rapid task orientation, structural stability, and dignified calm.

The overarching aesthetic blends **Corporate/Modern** government architecture with high-empathy functionalism:
- **Trustworthy & Authoritative:** Anchored in official Philippine government branding, incorporating standard gov.ph mastheads, legible typography, and strict visual accountability.
- **Crisis-First Empathy:** Low cognitive load, high contrast, prominent plain-language guidance, and unambiguous feedback states designed to serve citizens across diverse digital literacy levels and device capabilities.
- **Accessible & Transparent:** Adheres strictly to WCAG 2.1 Level AA standards, guaranteeing that critical social welfare services remain legible, operable, and resilient under degraded network connectivity.

## Colors

The color palette draws directly from the official DSWD identity and Philippine national motifs, engineered for optimal contrast against light backgrounds.

### Core Swatches
- **Primary Deep Blue (`#0D3B85`):** Serves as the primary brand anchor, dominant action color, active navigational headers, and structural institutional accents.
- **Secondary Action Blue (`#1A56DB`):** Utilized for interactive states, hover tiers, active tabs, and secondary links.
- **National Gold (`#D97706`):** Applied deliberately to persistent notifications, warnings, pending statuses, and critical focal elements without overwhelming the primary blue.
- **Crisis Crimson Accent (`#DC2626`):** Utilized strictly for critical system alerts, validation errors, and urgent document requirements.

### Status Tokens
- **Success / Approved:** Background `#DCFCE7`, Border `#86EFAC`, Foreground `#15803D`.
- **Pending / In Review:** Background `#FEF3C7`, Border `#FDE68A`, Foreground `#B45309`.
- **Action Required / Rejected:** Background `#FEE2E2`, Border `#FCA5A5`, Foreground `#B91C1C`.
- **Informational / Processing:** Background `#E0F2FE`, Border `#BAE6FD`, Foreground `#0284C7`.

### Surface & Neutral Architecture
- Canvas Background: `#F8FAFC`
- Card & Panel Surface: `#FFFFFF`
- Structural Hairlines & Borders: `#E2E8F0`
- Subtle Fill / Disabled States: `#F1F5F9`
- Text Heavy (Headings/Body): `#0F172A`
- Text Muted (Captions/Meta): `#475569`

## Typography

The design system exclusively implements **Inter** across all display, body, and interface tokens to ensure optimal readability, high x-height rendering on budget mobile displays, and broad language symbol support.

- **Legibility Over Ornamentation:** Line-height is intentionally generous (minimum 1.45x for headings, 1.5x for body text) to accommodate English and Filipino instructions without text collision or crowding.
- **Hierarchy Enforcement:** Section titles and critical form groupings utilize strict weight shifts (`FontWeight 600` and `700`) rather than decorative serif stylings, preserving systematic clarity throughout multi-step forms.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model utilizes a structured, 12-column responsive fluid grid pinned to an institutional maximum container width of `1280px`.

### Responsive Breakpoints & Rhythm
- **Mobile (`< 640px`):** 4-column layout, `margin-mobile: 1rem` (`16px`), `gutter-mobile: 1rem` (`16px`). Sidebars collapse into accessible off-canvas drawers; form steps stack vertically.
- **Tablet (`640px - 1024px`):** 8-column layout, `margin: 1.5rem` (`24px`), `gutter: 1.5rem` (`24px`). Form sections adapt into stacked 2-column input fields.
- **Desktop (`> 1024px`):** 12-column layout, `margin: 2rem` (`32px`), `gutter: 1.5rem` (`24px`). Persistent application sidebar occupying 3 columns, central content/form area spanning 9 columns.

### Vertical Rhythm
Interface components adhere strictly to an 8px spatial grid (`space-xs` = 4px, `space-sm` = 8px, `space-md` = 16px, `space-lg` = 24px, `space-xl` = 32px), eliminating random alignment offsets and ensuring high mechanical consistency.

## Elevation & Depth

To maintain government-grade legibility and assist users navigating intense personal situations, depth is handled primarily through **tonal surfaces** and **crisp 1px low-contrast structural outlines**, supplemented by gentle ambient shadows. Heavy skeuomorphism and glossy blurs are omitted.

### Elevation Levels
- **Level 0 (Flat / Canvas):** Applied to the main page canvas (`#F8FAFC`). No shadow, no border.
- **Level 1 (Card / Resting Surface):** Applied to standard content cards, fieldset containers, and list rows. Pure white surface (`#FFFFFF`), solid border `1px solid #E2E8F0`, and an ambient diffused shadow: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05)`.
- **Level 2 (Active / Hover / Dropdown):** Applied to interactive card hovers, active accordions, and dropdown menus. Solid border `1px solid #CBD5E1`, elevated shadow: `0 4px 6px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.07), 0 2px 4px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05)`.
- **Level 3 (Modal / Persistent Status Drawers):** Applied to confirmation dialogs, urgent notification sheets, and sticky verification banners. Elevation shadow: `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.1), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06)`, framed with a neutral border `1px solid #CBD5E1`.

## Shapes

The design system establishes a **Soft (`1`)** corner language (`rounded = 0.25rem / 4px`, `rounded-lg = 0.5rem / 8px`, `rounded-xl = 0.75rem / 12px`).

- **Utility & Inputs:** Standard input fields, buttons, dropdown selects, and badge containers use `0.375rem` (`6px`) to retain crisp geometric structure and official presence without looking clinical.
- **Cards & Data Modules:** Application cards, upload zones, and summary surfaces employ `0.5rem` (`8px`).
- **Pill Exception:** Status indicators (e.g., "Pending Review", "Approved", "Requirements Lacking") utilize fully rounded pill shapes (`rounded-full`) to contrast visibly against rectilinear data cards.

## Components

### Gov.ph Global Header Bar
- A persistent 32px top strip in neutral slate (`#0F172A`) or primary dark blue (`#102A6B`) featuring the official Republic of the Philippines seal mark, "GOVPH", standard accessibility toggles (Text Size, High Contrast), and official Philippine Standard Time display.

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Solid `#0D3B85` background, white text (`#FFFFFF`), `font-weight: 600`, minimum touch target height `48px` on mobile, `44px` on desktop. Focus ring: `3px solid #93C5FD`. Hover state transitions to `#1A56DB`.
- **Secondary:** White background with `1.5px solid #0D3B85`, text `#0D3B85`.
- **Critical / Danger:** Solid `#DC2626` background with white text, or outline `#DC2626` for secondary destructive actions.

### Multi-Stage Crisis Application Stepper (Progress Tracker)
- A persistent multi-stage progress component displaying progress through the AICS service pipeline:
  1. Citizen Profile & Beneficiary Info
  2. Crisis Category Selection (Medical, Burial, Educational, Fire/Calamity)
  3. Documentary Requirements & Verification
  4. Assessment & Case Worker Scheduling
  5. Payout / Assistance Release
- **States:** Completed steps show a green circular badge (`#15803D`) with a checkmark; active step shows an institutional blue ring (`#0D3B85`) with bold text; upcoming steps remain subdued `#94A3B8`.

### Input Fields & Upload Controls
- Minimum height `44px`, border `1px solid #CBD5E1`, background `#FFFFFF`. Active focus state triggers `2px solid #0D3B85` with zero offset blur.
- Mandatory field indicators show a distinct crimson asterisk (`*`). Error messages are rendered directly beneath inputs with an accompanying warning icon and crimson text (`#B91C1C`).
- **File Upload Droppers:** High-visibility dashed boundary (`2px dashed #94A3B8`) with direct mobile-camera and gallery file pickers, displaying accepted formats (PDF, JPG, PNG) and max size per document (10MB).

### Badges & Status Chips
- Pill geometry with padding `4px 10px` and typography `label-sm`.
- Always paired with an icon (e.g., check-circle for approved, clock for pending, alert-circle for deficient requirements) to guarantee accessibility for color-blind citizens.

### Case Cards & Assistance Summaries
- Encapsulated within Level 1 surface depth. Header row contains the Reference Code (e.g., `AICS-2025-NCR-08492`), transaction date, and active status chip. Body details beneficiary name, assistance classification, and assigned DSWD Field Office.

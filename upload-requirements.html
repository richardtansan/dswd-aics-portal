Tips for Quick Approval

  • 1

    Corners & Lighting: Ensure all four corners of the physical document are in frame, free of shadows or reflective flash glare.

  • 2

    Doctor's License & Seals: The physician's PRC license number and official hospital dry seal/stamp must be sharp and legible.

  • 3

    Multi-page Billing: PDF format is strongly recommended for itemized hospital bills with more than 2 pages to prevent missing sheets.
